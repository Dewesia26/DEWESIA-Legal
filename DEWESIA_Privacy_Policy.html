DEWESIA Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

DEWESIA is an educational application that helps users practice writing, speech, and poetry. This policy describes how information may be handled when using the application.

Information we collect

DEWESIA uses email-based accounts. When you register or sign in, your email address and account authentication information are processed to provide account access. If you provide a name as part of your account, that information may also be processed. Text and projects you enter in the application may be stored on your device or, where cloud saving is enabled, in the application's database.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate accounts, operate the application, support its writing features, maintain saved work where applicable, and respond to support or privacy requests. DEWESIA does not use account email addresses for targeted advertising.

Service providers and storage

DEWESIA uses Supabase for account authentication and database services. Information processed through these services is subject to appropriate technical and organizational protections provided by the service and the application's configuration. Data may be processed on servers operated by the service provider.

Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by service providers necessary to operate DEWESIA, or disclosed when required by applicable law.

Data retention and deletion

Account information and cloud-stored projects may be retained while an account remains active or as needed to operate the service. You may request access to or deletion of your personal information by contacting the email below. We will review and respond to requests, subject to applicable legal or operational requirements. Clearing a project inside the app may not automatically delete account records.

Children's privacy

DEWESIA is an educational writing tool. Parents or guardians with questions about a child's use of the app or requests concerning their child's information can contact us using the address below.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information, including using authentication and database access controls. No electronic system can guarantee absolute security.

Changes

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the app or applicable requirements. The effective date above will be updated when changes are made.

Contact
